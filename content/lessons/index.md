# Lessons

Ten hands-on notebooks. Each one starts from a planning question and ends with a map or chart that answers it.

You can do the lessons in any order, because every notebook downloads its own data.
For complete beginners, though, going in order works best.

## Part 1: GIS with OpenStreetMap

1. [Your Study Area](01_study_area.md): city boundary, wards and your first maps
2. [Mapping the City from OpenStreetMap](02_osm_city_map.md): buildings, roads, parks and amenities
3. [Street Network Analysis](03_street_network.md): intersection density, connectivity and street orientation
4. [The 15-Minute City](04_15min_city.md): walking access to parks and schools
5. [Urban Form and Buildings](05_urban_form.md): footprints, ground coverage and building shapes

## Part 2: Remote sensing with satellite images

6. [Satellite Imagery Basics](06_satellite_basics.md): finding and viewing Sentinel-2 images
7. [Green Cover and Built-up Areas](07_green_builtup.md): NDVI and NDBI by ward
8. [Urban Heat Island](08_urban_heat.md): land surface temperature from Landsat
9. [Urban Growth with Machine Learning](09_urban_growth.md): land cover classification and change over time

## Part 3: Bringing it together

10. [Site Suitability (Capstone)](10_site_suitability.md): where to put a new park, shared as an interactive web map

## Bonus material

- [Collecting Citizen Data with ODK and KoboToolbox](../bonus/citizen_data_odk_kobo.md): a no-code guide that takes you from a phone survey to a public map
