Building footprints reveal the character of a place. You will compare Jayanagar and Shivajinagar with figure-ground maps, building shape measures and a ground coverage grid.

## What you will learn

- Make figure-ground maps from OSM footprints
- Measure footprint area, compactness, elongation and orientation with momepy
- Calculate ground coverage on a 100 m grid
- Compare the morphology of two neighbourhoods

## Key concepts

- **Urban morphology**: the study of the form of a city
- **Ground coverage**: the share of land covered by buildings
- **Shape indices**: numbers that describe building form

## What you will make

Figure-ground maps and ground coverage heat maps for the two areas.
